- **$P(Z>TS_{cal})+P(Z<-TS_{cal})$** for two tailed hypothesis 
- $\uparrow\alpha$ --> $\downarrow\beta$
- $\downarrow\alpha$ --> $\uparrow\beta$ 
- We **can't increase/decrease** both errors ($\alpha,\beta$) at the same time

	- $n\uparrow$ --> $(\alpha,\beta)\downarrow$
